Before and after

Today

Scattered, and money left behind

  • You find out what exists by Googling, or from a hospital social worker. If you don't research, you have no clue.
  • You call around. No one explains your options, your costs, or your future eligibility.
  • You repeat your story to each worker; the forms are long and full of jargon.
  • Income-tested programs need figures you can't recall, and the DTC gateway that unlocks the Canada Disability Benefit and the RDSP is invisible.
  • Result: benefits go unclaimed and care gets delayed.
With Covered

One path, in plain language

  • One screen, or upload your Notice of Assessment and we read your income so you skip the questions.
  • You see every program you may qualify for, with the unlock chain (DTC → CDB → RDSP) surfaced for you.
  • Deterministic, cited, drift-checked rules: numbers you can trust, each with its official source.
  • Real next steps and prepared forms. You confirm and submit; nothing is filed automatically.
  • Built for busy people: large text, a natural voice, tap Yes/No, upload instead of type.

What's real, and what's demo

So you can trust what you're testing.

✓ Real
  • The rules engine: deterministic, auditable, 99 tests, cited figures.
  • The Notice of Assessment reader (income extracted; SIN never stored).
  • The policy drift checker: re-reads official pages and flags changed figures.
  • SurrealDB persistence with two-layer row-level security.
⚠ Demo only
  • Sign-in is off in this public demo; auth is architected and fails closed in production.
  • The agency console reads a simulated CRM (synthetic records), not real client data.
  • Nothing is ever filed to the CRA or a program; Covered prepares, you submit.
